How to play

  • Each clue asks for a movie, like Name a horror movie. Type any film that fits. There are no suggestions, but typos and missing punctuation are fine.
  • You get 30 seconds per clue and as many guesses as you like. The clock starts when you press Start.
  • Any correct film scores. The less obvious your pick, the more it's worth, and each clue hides one 100-point jackpot film.
  • A new daily arrives every midnight. Play every day to build a streak and unlock new looks for your statuette.
